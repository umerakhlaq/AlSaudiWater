Understanding Your Water

What These Values Mean

General information about water quality parameters.

pH Level

pH measures how acidic or basic the water is. A pH of 7 is neutral. Values between 6.5–8.5 are generally considered acceptable for drinking water.

TDS (Total Dissolved Solids)

TDS represents the total concentration of dissolved substances in water, measured in mg/L. It includes minerals, salts, and metals.

Mineral Content

Calcium, magnesium, sodium and other minerals appear naturally in water. The values shown reflect the analysis from the product label.
